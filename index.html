Torque

Working machines for Minecraft Bedrock. Power flows through shafts and cogwheels from a source to your machines, and everything it drives turns, slows and jams the way a real mechanism would.

  • Version 1.0.1, finished
  • 650+ blocks and items
  • Stable Script API only, no experimental toggles
  • Every texture, model and sound in the pack is our own

The film is rendered from the add-on's own block models on a set of Minecraft block textures © Mojang AB. Not in-game footage.

Running in game

In-game footage from Torque 1.0.1, recorded at 2560 × 1440, 60 fps.

  • Make power

    Water wheels, windmills, steam engines and a hand crank.

  • Carry it

    Shafts, cogwheels, gearboxes, clutches and chain drives change speed and direction and split power.

  • Put it to work

    Presses, mixers, millstones, saws, drills, fans and deployers turn raw materials into finished goods.

  • Move things

    Belts, funnels, chutes and vaults carry items between machines. Pipes, pumps and tanks move fluids.

  • Build things that move

    Bearings, pistons and pulleys move whole structures. Lay track, add stations and signals, and run trains.
